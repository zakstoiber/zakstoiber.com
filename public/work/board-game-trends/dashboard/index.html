About the data

Every ranked game on BoardGameGeek (30+ user ratings) from an August 2026 pull, with its mechanism and category tags. Min. user ratings sets how many ratings a game needs to count everywhere on this page; 100+ is the default because a handful of ratings is noisy, and it matches the earlier solo analysis.

Trends run 1980–2025: 2026 is a partial year, and recent games are still collecting ratings. Games with no weight votes are left out of anything that uses weight. Rating is the average user rating, not BGG's adjusted geek rating.

Crowdfunding tags (BoardGameGeek's “Crowdfunding:” families) and the suggested-player-count poll come from a second pull, in October 2026. The tags are kept by BGG users, so some crowdfunded games are missing them.

Designers come from the same October pull. A co-designed game counts for each of its designers, and uncredited games are left out.

Rating spread (the standard deviation of each game's user ratings) comes from a third October pull. Divisive compares that spread with what games of similar average rating, weight, popularity and release era show, fit at each ratings floor, and gives where the difference falls among games at that floor: top 10% means a game's ratings are more spread out than 90% of them, and bottom 10% means they agree more than 90% of them do. The small figure after a rating, such as top 5% or bottom 30%, is where it falls among all games at the floor; after an adjusted rating, it is where that falls among every game's adjusted rating with the same adjustments.

Collection counts (copies owned and wishlisted) and the player-age poll come from the same third pull. Series are BoardGameGeek family tags that start with “Series:”, from the second pull.
